does Jev understand colour?

Type a word. It goes to Jev, TypeSafe AI's System One model, as a single typed question with sixteen options. Jev cannot write a word back: it returns a probability for each option. The bar is those probabilities, widest first. Nothing else decides anything — there is no palette logic, no colour maths, no second model. Hover a band to see which colour it is and how much Jev gave it.

The sixteen colours are borrowed, with thanks: they are the palette published as docs/palette.md in Matt DesLauriers' Bitframes, a generative-art project of his from 2024, MIT licensed. Sixteen colour values are all this page takes; Bitframes itself is an artwork encoded in 32 bytes and has nothing to do with language models.

The page exists because of his demo on 18 September 2026 and his own description of how it worked — sixteen predefined colours, "just rendering their weighted probabilities". He linked no source, so everything here is written from that sentence and is mine, including any part of it that is wrong. He has no involvement in this page and has not endorsed it; it is not affiliated with TypeSafe AI either.
